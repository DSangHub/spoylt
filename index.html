How SPOYLT works

Three simple steps from frustration to collective action.

1

Start a Proposition

Rally Support

3

Put Them on Notice

Public officials get a dedicated space to respond, engage, and be held accountable. Silence is no longer free.

Submit a candidate video

Verified candidates may supply an MP4 for review. SPOYLT verifies the candidate, sponsor, content, and requested ZIP before placement. Submission does not publish the video.

Videos remain private during automated speech screening and human review. Automatic speech screening supports MP4 files up to 25 MB and checks foul language and conflicting identity claims. SPOYLT also reviews the person shown or heard, on-screen text, campaign authorization, legal disclosure, and ZIP placement before publication.

Automatic FAQ assistant

Verified Premium officials and candidates can authorize routine factual replies. Add up to 20 FAQs with public sources. AI selects an approved answer without rewriting it. Advocacy, personalized political persuasion, sensitive cases and uncovered questions require human handling.

Private question inbox

Ask an official or candidate’s AI assistant

Enabled assistants answer routine factual questions using approved FAQs. Every automatic answer is labeled as AI. Questions without an approved answer go to the account owner for review. This is a private exchange, not a public post.
