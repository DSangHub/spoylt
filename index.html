How SPOYLT works

Three simple steps from frustration to collective action.

1

Start a Proposition

Pick an issue that affects your community — housing, gas prices, potholes, fire protection, police, animal services, food banks — and write your suggestion in plain language.

2

Rally Support

Your Spoylt goes live on the Community Board. Neighbors like, comment, and share. Momentum is visible. Lawmakers can see the heat.

3

Put Them on Notice

Public officials get a dedicated space to respond, engage, and be held accountable. Silence is no longer free.
